Privacy Policy

How the AutoRentBoss Android application handles rental business information, personal records, backups, advertising, and privacy choices.

Effective: October 20, 2026 · Application: AutoRentBoss (Android)

1. Who we are and the scope of this policy

AutoRentBoss (“the app”) is a car-rental management application developed and maintained under the AutoRentBoss name. It is intended for rental business operators managing vehicles, reservations, renters, payments, deposits, agreements, and returns.

This policy describes data handled by the app and its integrated services. The rental business using AutoRentBoss determines what customer information it enters, why it needs that information, and how long it retains its business records. Renters seeking access to records held by a particular rental operator should contact that operator directly.

The accompanying website is a static information site; it does not ask visitors to create an account or submit rental records. The website's hosting provider may collect ordinary technical access logs.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on what the operator enters or captures, AutoRentBoss can handle:

  • Business details: rental business name, representative, contact information, business address, registration information, and tax identification information.
  • Vehicles: make, model, plate number, mileage, pricing, registration dates, condition photos, maintenance history, and OR/CR or other vehicle documents.
  • Renters and drivers: names, phone or other contact details, primary-driver information, driver's-license images and extracted details, and supporting identification or documents.
  • Reservations and rentals: booking schedules, status, checkout/return condition records, captured photos, signatures, signed agreements, and related notes.
  • Financial records: rental charges, discounts, manual payment records, payment references or methods, security deposits, deductions, refunds, and reports.
  • Technical information: limited device and app information needed for optional developer-access verification, and information processed by Google's advertising and consent SDKs as described below.

The app is not a bank or payment processor. Recording a GCash, Maya, cash, bank, or card payment in the app does not mean AutoRentBoss itself transfers money or obtains the user's bank login or card security code.

3. Local-first storage

Core rental business records are stored in a local SQLite database and app-managed file storage on the operator's device. In the implementation covered by this policy, the app does not automatically upload the operator's rental database, renter identification documents, vehicle documents, or signatures to an AutoRentBoss-managed cloud database.

Local-first does not mean no network traffic. Google's advertising, consent, and Play services may communicate with their own servers. Information deliberately exported or shared by the operator may also leave the device.

System backup is disabled: the current Android app sets android:allowBackup="false" and specifies data-extraction rules excluding app data from Android system/cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Operators can instead create and separately store password-protected encrypted .arbbackup files using the app's manual Backup & Restore feature. Files deliberately exported or shared may still be retained by the destination provider or recipient.

4. Camera, photos, and documents

With permission, AutoRentBoss uses the device camera or supported Android document-scanning features to capture vehicle photos, condition evidence, registration documents, driver's licenses, and other documents. The app may also allow selection of images or PDF files from device storage.

Google ML Kit / Google Play services may assist with document scanning and text recognition, including reading license details from selected images. The scanned results and confirmed records are saved to app-managed storage. Temporary copies are used during some workflows and are removed where the implementation supports cleanup.

Camera access is requested for relevant capture workflows rather than to record continuously. AutoRentBoss does not include continuous GPS vehicle tracking in the current app.

5. Advertising, consent, and Google services

AutoRentBoss integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK for banner advertising on eligible Free-tier app screens, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. An advertisement loading failure does not cancel or reverse a rental or payment operation.

Google's advertising and consent services may automatically process or share technical information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and privacy preferences, such as:

  • IP address, which may indicate approximate location;
  • app and device details, advertising identifiers where available, and other device/account identifiers;
  • ad interactions and app-use events relevant to advertising; and
  • diagnostic and performance information, plus consent choices and signals.

Depending on region and consent, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. AutoRentBoss does not intentionally attach renter names, driver's-license images, reservation records, signatures, or the local rental database to its ad requests.

Where required, UMP presents a consent message. The app also provides an Ad Privacy entry under More to reopen applicable privacy options. Device-level advertising preferences can also be changed through Android settings.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

6. Internet access and third-party services

The app requests internet/network permissions for Google Play Billing, advertising and consent services and Free-tier connectivity checks. In commercial Android Release builds, Free operators need an internet connection to create new vehicles or new reservations; existing rental operations, returns, financial records, exports and manual backups remain available without that requirement. Premium has additional offline capabilities under a locally cached eligibility allowance. Google's SDKs may communicate with Google servers. AutoRentBoss does not operate a customer-facing cloud account or automatically upload the local rental database.

Google Play subscriptions: AutoRentBoss offers an optional, automatically renewing Premium subscription with monthly and annual billing plans purchased through Google's Play Billing system. Available prices, renewals, payment handling, cancellation and subscription management are provided by Google Play. The app reads matching Play subscription ownership on the device, caches limited local eligibility timestamps in Android secure storage, and acknowledges eligible completed purchases after granting access. It does not store Google Play purchase tokens in the rental database or operate its own payment processor. It does not currently use a custom subscription-verification server or real-time developer notifications; offline access and delayed network checks therefore cannot guarantee instantaneous reflection of refunds, subscription expiry, or revocations. Users can open Google Play's subscription management page from My Plan.

The optional Support Development screen may display a contribution destination for the operator to use separately. Viewing or copying it does not transmit business records to the recipient through the app, and contributions do not grant Premium access.

7. Backup, export, restore, and sharing

AutoRentBoss can create password-protected encrypted .arbbackup exports containing its local database and managed supporting files. The app uses authenticated encryption for these operator-created archives; the backup password is needed for restoration.

Operators may export reports or agreements, save backups to Android Downloads, or use the Android file picker, share sheet, or another installed application to send files to a chosen recipient or storage provider. Once the operator shares a file, that recipient or provider is responsible for its subsequent handling under their own terms and privacy practices.

Protect exported documents and backup passwords. Backups and reports may contain personal data. Uninstalling AutoRentBoss does not automatically remove files previously saved to Downloads, shared externally, or retained by system backup services.

8. Data retention and deletion

Rental, customer, vehicle, payment, and document records remain in local storage until the operator changes or removes them using an available app workflow, clears app data, or uninstalls the app. Certain completed business and financial records may be retained as historical evidence and cannot be deleted individually through the current interface.

Clearing app data or uninstalling typically removes its local private storage from the device, but does not necessarily delete copies already exported, shared or stored manually with third parties. Android's automatic backup of this app is disabled in the current manifest; operators remain responsible for deleting their own exported archives and documents where they saved them. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's own retention and deletion procedures.

AutoRentBoss currently does not require operators to register a cloud account, so there is no separate AutoRentBoss cloud account to delete in this version. For customer or driver records maintained by an operator, contact the relevant rental business to request correction or deletion, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations.

9. Security

AutoRentBoss uses app-managed private file storage, input validation, transaction safeguards for rental and financial records, controlled document viewing, and password-based encryption for operator-created backup archives. These protections reduce risk, but no application, device, network, or storage system is completely secure.

Important distinction: encryption of exported backups does not mean that every copy of the active local SQLite database is encrypted at rest. Operators should secure their device with a screen lock, keep Android and Google Play services updated, use strong backup passwords, and share documents only with authorized recipients.

10. Privacy choices and requests

Operators can control which business records they enter, whether to capture optional photos or documents, whether to export or share data, and (where applicable) advertising privacy options. Android permissions can be managed in device settings.

If your request concerns information entered by a specific car-rental operator, that operator is the appropriate first contact because AutoRentBoss does not run a centralized renter database. For questions about the app or advertising integration, use the developer contact methods below.

11. Children

AutoRentBoss is a business productivity tool intended for rental operators. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly design the app to solicit information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if subscription verification, third-party SDKs, cloud features, or other data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published. Please review the current policy and Google Play Data safety declarations together.

13. Contact and privacy inquiries

App/developer: AutoRentBoss

For general privacy policy or app-support inquiries, submit a request through the AutoRentBoss website support/issues page. GitHub issues are public: do not post renter names, identification images, contact numbers, backup files, or other private information there.

For private privacy requests, use the developer support email listed on the AutoRentBoss Google Play listing once available. If your inquiry concerns records maintained by a rental operator, contact that operator directly.

Scope: This policy reflects the Android application source reviewed on October 20, 2026. Before public release, the developer must ensure that Google Play Data safety declarations, SDK data collection disclosures, and the private support contact listed in Play Console remain accurate.

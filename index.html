04 / THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What to know before putting your rental business in motion.

Is AutoRentBoss a car-booking marketplace?+

No. AutoRentBoss is a business management app for car-rental operators. It helps you manage your own vehicles, reservations and rental operations; it is not a public marketplace for customers to book cars.

Can I use AutoRentBoss without internet?+

Premium supports offline access subject to the app's subscription entitlement checks. Free requires an internet connection for regular use.

Where does my business data live?+

Business records are stored on your device. AutoRentBoss also supports encrypted manual backups that you can save and restore. Read our privacy policy for details.

How do I upgrade to Premium?+

Open My Plan inside AutoRentBoss on Android. Plans, regional availability and subscription checkout are handled by Google Play.

Does AutoRentBoss support rental agreements?+

Yes. The app supports agreement templates, rental-specific agreement snapshots, signatures, and PDF sharing or printing.
